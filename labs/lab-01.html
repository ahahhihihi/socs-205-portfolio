Exploring Urban Green Spaces in Shanghai

The question

Where are the major green spaces located within Shanghai's urban core?

The data

OpenStreetMap data (2024) showing parks and green spaces in Shanghai. The data is open-source under the Open Database License. Missing: private green spaces, rooftop gardens, and small pocket parks not mapped in OSM.

The method

Used QGIS to filter OpenStreetMap data for leisure=park features within Shanghai's administrative boundaries. Projection: WGS 84 (EPSG:4326). Simple visualization with green fill to highlight park areas.

What I found

Shanghai has significant green space coverage, particularly along the Huangpu River and in western districts like Changning and Xuhui. The distribution shows planning efforts to integrate nature into urban areas.

What this does not show

This map only shows publicly accessible parks from OpenStreetMap. It doesn't include private gardens, rooftop green spaces, or small community gardens. The data quality varies by neighborhood, and newer developments may not be fully mapped.
